# Late Checkout Policy

## Standard Late Checkout

Standard checkout time is 11:00.

Guests may request a late checkout subject to hotel occupancy and housekeeping availability.

Late checkout until 13:00 may be granted free of charge when availability permits.

## Extended Late Checkout

Checkout between 13:00 and 17:00 may incur a half-day room charge.

Checkout after 17:00 is normally charged as a full additional night.

## VIP Guests

VIP guests should receive priority consideration for late checkout requests.

The front desk employee must verify availability with housekeeping before confirming the request.

## Important

Never guarantee late checkout before checking room availability and housekeeping status.
