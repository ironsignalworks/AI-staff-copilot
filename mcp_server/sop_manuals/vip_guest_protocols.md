# VIP Guest Protocols

## VIP Identification

VIP guests include guests designated by management or identified in the hotel's reservation system.

## Arrival

VIP guests should receive priority during check-in.

The front desk should notify the duty manager when a VIP guest arrives.

## Room Requests

VIP requests should be handled with priority when operationally possible.

Employees must not promise complimentary services unless the service is authorized by hotel policy or management.

## Complaints

VIP complaints should be escalated to the duty manager immediately.
